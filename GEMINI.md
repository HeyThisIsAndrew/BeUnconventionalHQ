# Workspace Rules

- **Clean up after yourself**: Do not leave any lingering scratch files, `.log` files, temporary Node scripts, or any other temporary artifacts in the workspace at the end of development runs or after resolving issues. Explicitly delete them before completing your task. If a scratch file must be preserved temporarily for your own internal context, store it exclusively in the Antigravity `scratch/` directory within the artifacts folder, never in the project workspace.
